## Brand & Style

This design system is tailored for high-stakes, competitive pitch environments—specifically college and national hackathon stages where technical sophistication and product maturity must be communicated within seconds. The tone balances enterprise-grade software credibility with an inviting, civic-oriented warmth. 

Rather than leaning into generic ultra-dark tech tropes or stark clinic whites, the visual language utilizes an editorial warm-earth palette anchored by deep espresso, mocha structural elements, and ambient champagne surfaces. The overall aesthetic merges modern SaaS UI precision (crisp data visualizations, disciplined modular cards, state-driven workflow steppers) with tactile presentation polish. The visual narrative signals dependability, operational readiness, and high aesthetic execution to jury panels and venture judges alike.

## Colors

The color system establishes functional hierarchy tailored for 16:9 high-luminance projectors and displays:

- **Base Canvas (`#FFFAF2` - Warm Ivory):** Replaces harsh standard white, mitigating eye strain and delivering an elevated, premium presentation ground.
- **Primary Structural Accent (`#6F4E37` - Mocha Brown):** Drives foundational typography, persistent slide navbars, modal frames, and high-level card boundaries.
- **Interactive Call-to-Action (`#BF6B2E` - Burnt Amber):** High-energy primary accent reserved exclusively for critical action triggers, focal interactive nodes, active workflow indicators, and key metrics.
- **Surface Highlight (`#F7E7CE` - Champagne Gold):** Deployed across feature callouts, active card backgrounds, and emphasized table rows.
- **Text & High-Contrast Foreground (`#34251F` - Dark Espresso):** High-legibility anchor for body copy, metric digits, and dense dashboard contents.
- **Borders & Dividers (`#D8C3A5` - Soft Taupe):** Subtle structural boundaries ensuring clear definition between adjacent modules without cognitive weight.
- **Semantic Feedback:**
  - **Success / Approved (`#879078` - Muted Sage):** Used for healthy resource counts, confirmed allocations, and completed stages.
  - **Alert / Pending (`#A94438` - Muted Brick):** Used for bottlenecks, urgent requests, depleted stock, and attention-required flags.

## Typography

Typography establishes an immediate hierarchy across macro slide storytelling and micro dashboard layouts:

- **Display & Headlines (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Geometric yet friendly contours project an approachable, product-forward feel. Display scales (`display-slide` and `headline-xl`) carry tighter negative tracking to preserve compactness and punch across projected keynotes.
- **Interface Metrics (`metric-display`):** Heavyweight numbers designed for big data callouts (e.g., "$1.2M Saved", "99.4% Fulfillment").
- **Body & Captions (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Retains crisp legibility in paragraph layouts and summary bullet cards.
- **Labels, Badges, and Data Tables (Inter):** Systematically applied to status pills, navigation indices, table columns, and form inputs to maximize dense data parsing and clarity.

## Layout & Spacing

The system enforces a dual-purpose layout engine: a 16:9 presentation slide canvas (1920×1080 native scale) hosting an embedded 12-column responsive SaaS product grid.

- **Presentation Canvas (16:9):** Uses a disciplined outer breathing room of `margin-deck` (64px / 4rem) and horizontal card column gaps of `gutter-deck` (40px / 2.5rem). Slides organize content into three primary archetypes:
  1. *Split Narrative (60/40):* Left side handles the narrative pitch, thesis, and core metric; right side holds the interactive SaaS workflow preview.
  2. *Tri-Card Architecture:* 3 equal columns spanning 4 grid units each for feature, persona, or validation breakdowns.
  3. *Full-Bleed Product Showcase:* Single focus mockup spanning 12 columns with peripheral telemetry metrics.
- **Component Layout:** Nested cards, dashboards, and form containers strictly operate on a standard 8pt spatial baseline (`space-xs` through `space-2xl`). Consistent internal padding keeps complex data modules easily digestible for quick-glance jury comprehension.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is conveyed through a layered warm-substrate architecture rather than heavy drop shadows:

- **Layer 0 (Canvas Base):** Solid `#FFFAF2` (Warm Ivory).
- **Layer 1 (Card & Module Foundation):** `#FFFFFF` surfaces bounded by a 1px solid `#D8C3A5` (Soft Taupe) border. Shadows are ambient and tinted: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(52, 37, 31, 0.04)`.
- **Layer 2 (Highlighted & Interactive Surfaces):** Solid `#F7E7CE` (Champagne Gold) cards or raised floating overlays. Shadows deepen slightly: `0 8px 30px -4px rgba(52, 37, 31, 0.08)`.
- **Layer 3 (Modals, Popovers & Contextual Stepper Menus):** Pure white surfaces with a micro-border `rgba(111, 78, 55, 0.15)` and an ambient diffuse shadow: `0 16px 40px -8px rgba(52, 37, 31, 0.14)`.
- **Glass Accents:** Translucent elements (such as persistent floating slide decks or dashboard filters) use `rgba(255, 250, 242, 0.82)` paired with a `12px` backdrop blur and an outline of `1px solid rgba(216, 195, 165, 0.5)`.

## Shapes

The design system maintains a balanced `Rounded` (Level 2) shape hierarchy. This softens analytical dashboard elements while retaining structural SaaS discipline:

- **Standard Containers & Cards:** `0.5rem` (8px) border radius for compact UI widgets, increasing to `1rem` (16px) for major presentation cards.
- **Workflow Steppers & Hero Cards:** `1.5rem` (24px) border radius for distinct focal sections.
- **Form Inputs & Action Buttons:** Uniform `0.5rem` (8px) radius to maintain high visual alignment with embedded input fields.
- **Badges, Tags, and Stepper Nodes:** Fully pill-shaped (`9999px`) for quick visual segmentation and status scanning.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary CTA:** Solid Burnt Amber (`#BF6B2E`) background with high-contrast Warm Ivory (`#FFFAF2`) text. 8px radius, semi-bold Inter typography, 10px 20px padding. Hover state: slight brightness lift with a subtle shadow expansion.
- **Secondary Action:** Transparent base with a 1.5px Mocha Brown (`#6F4E37`) outline and Mocha Brown text.
- **Tertiary / Sub-action:** Soft Champagne Gold (`#F7E7CE`) fill with Mocha Brown text and zero border.

### Badges & Status Indicators
- **Approved / Active:** Muted Sage fill at 15% opacity (`rgba(135, 144, 120, 0.15)`), solid Muted Sage text (`#879078`), and a 6px solid dot indicator.
- **Pending / Action Required:** Muted Brick fill at 15% opacity (`rgba(169, 68, 56, 0.15)`), solid Muted Brick text (`#A94438`), accompanied by an alert icon.
- **Neutral Category:** Champagne Gold (`#F7E7CE`) fill with Mocha Brown (`#6F4E37`) text.

### Workflow Stepper
- A horizontal multi-stage path tracking real-time handoffs:
  - *Completed Step:* Mocha Brown circle with a white checkmark, linked by a solid 2px Mocha Brown path.
  - *Active Step:* Burnt Amber outer ring with a pulsing center pip, linked to adjacent cards.
  - *Upcoming Step:* Soft Taupe border with an empty canvas fill.

### Cards & Dashboard Panels
- **Standard Card:** Clean white backdrop, 1px `#D8C3A5` border, rounded-lg corners, padded by `space-lg` (24px).
- **Featured Card:** Champagne Gold (`#F7E7CE`) background, Mocha Brown accents, framing value propositions or key judge takeaways.
- **Metric Highlight Card:** Top-bordered with a 4px Burnt Amber line, housing a 56px bold figure, micro-label, and positive trend pill.

### Form Inputs & Filters
- Clean white field with a Soft Taupe (`#D8C3A5`) border. Active focus transitions to a 1.5px Mocha Brown border with an amber tinted focus-ring (`rgba(191, 107, 46, 0.2)`).

### Presentation Deck Components
- **Slide Category Header:** Uppercase 11px Inter label with 0.08em letter spacing, prefixed by an amber slash (`// MARKET OPPORTUNITY`).
- **Telemetry Bar:** Top or bottom utility dock displaying the hackathon team identifier, current slide step counter, and live platform demo timestamp.
